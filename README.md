# Russian equipment losses and front-line weather

Data Acquisition and Preprocessing project, TU Dublin.

Combines daily Russian equipment and personnel losses (as reported by Ukraine) with
weather observations scraped for occupied Donetsk and Kharkiv, to examine whether
loss patterns vary with weather conditions.

## Data sources
- **Kaggle:** 2022 Russia Ukraine War (piterfm) – daily cumulative equipment and personnel losses
- **Scraped:** pogodaiklimat.ru – 3-hourly weather observations (in progress)
- **Extension:** daily combat engagements reported by Ukraine's General Staff, via ukraine.vefgreining.com

## Repository contents
- `data/raw/` – original data files, unmodified
- `ai_usage_report.md` – log of AI prompts and reflection

*Status: in progress.*
